Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

At Travnook Travel and Tourism, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our visa consultation and travel assistance services.

1

Information We Collect

Personal Data: We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, such as your full name, email address, phone number, nationality, and supporting document files necessary for visa applications and embassy appointments.

Usage Data: Non-personal data collected automatically, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent, and referral sources, solely to analyze traffic trends and Google Ads performance.

2

How We Use Your Data

  • To process travel and visa assistance requests.
  • To contact you about your inquiries or CRM bookings.
  • To comply with regulatory legal requirements in the UAE.
  • To analyze and optimize search and Google Ads campaigns.
3

Data Security & Discretion

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS) to protect your personal details and travel documents. All submitted data is treated with the highest confidentiality and discretion.

4

Anti-Spam & WhatsApp Policy

By submitting our forms, you consent to receive informational messages about your visa assistance via WhatsApp. You can opt out at any time by replying 'Unsubscribe' or emailing contact@travnook.com.

5

Your Legal Rights

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection, you have the right to access, correct, erase, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Contact us at contact@travnook.com to exercise these rights.
