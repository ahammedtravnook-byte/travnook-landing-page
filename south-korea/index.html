FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a South Korea visa from Dubai?

The average processing duration at the official KVAC center in Dubai is 5 to 7 working days once submitted. It is highly recommended to plan and schedule at least 3-4 weeks in advance of your flight.

Do UAE residents require a visa for South Korea?

Yes, many nationalities residing in the UAE require a visa to enter South Korea. Only citizens of visa-exempt nations may travel with an approved K-ETA. Our agents verify your eligibility criteria before starting.

What is the required bank statement minimum?

The embassy requires a 3-month stamped bank statement. While there is no official minimum, having a stable balance that covers all your accommodation and itinerary costs (approx. 15,000 AED or more) is highly recommended.
